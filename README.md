# Recitroc

Recitroc is a goods and services bartering app based on user geolocation. The goal is to strengthen local community bonds through the exchange of goods and services. Users are encouraged to express their need and desire for specific goods and services. They are also to express what they may provide to the community. Recitroc's gamefication of theses processes is what makes it special. 

Bartering in the 21st century.

# Features

## Matching

During user onboarding, users are encouraged to express their needs from the community, be it a service like language conversation exchange, or goods like a bike or a sofa. This is done without estimation of a currency value becuase it goes against the spirit of barter and community building. So the matching is done through sophisticated algorithms that allow for the user to be matched with an individual with whom an exchange can take place.
